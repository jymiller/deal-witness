01

Growth Map

Watch meaning accumulate.

The Growth Map turns a long stream of records into a legible, left-to-right memory. It is designed to make scale visible without labeling every tiny node.

Evidence does not equal a new decision every time.

A new record may create a decision, reinforce one already remembered, or add provenance and context to an existing path.

Paths branch and converge.

One decision can expose several risks. Risks can lead to negotiated promises, and those promises can change how money moves.

Density is the point.

Repeated receipts make established paths more credible. The interface summarizes the corpus into 13 stable clusters so it remains readable.

Try it in 45 seconds.

  1. Choose Replay history to watch the map build from sparse evidence into a connected field.
  2. Choose Next document a few times. Watch a new receipt create or strengthen a path instead of moving the map around.
  3. Choose Highlight the open gap to isolate the hollow path that the second view will investigate.

Four states. One visual grammar.

Position tells you what kind of knowledge you are looking at. Shape, fill, and line tell you how well that knowledge is supported.

Heard once · hollow

Remembered, not resolved.

There is a receipt, claim, or open path worth preserving, but it has not reached the next support state.

Confirmed · solid

Supported by the loaded evidence.

The memory has enough attributed support to appear as confirmed in this fixed demonstration.

Written · in contract

Connected to the paper.

The remembered item is linked to contract wording in the document-check portion of the replay.

Missing · broken edge

The expected connection is absent.

A red broken line makes the gap visible without claiming the remembered fact itself has disappeared.

02

Recovery Explorer

Turn a gap into a sourced trail.

Maya is gone on Monday. The deal closes Friday. The Explorer demonstrates how historical receipts and relationship memory can help the remaining team recover context without pretending Maya is still available.

Maya is gone Monday. The $380M deal closes Friday.
01 · Expose

Find a hollow path.

The map identifies a specific unresolved connection rather than returning a generic “not found.”

02 · Prove

Show the receipts.

Conflicting or incomplete historical evidence explains exactly why the path remains open.

03 · Route

Rank who should know.

Authorship, attendance, ownership, approvals, and referrals support “Why this person?”

04 · Ask

Prepare a precise question.

The simulated outreach is grounded in the visible evidence. No real call or message is sent.

05 · Evaluate

Require a sourced answer.

A vague response stays open. A substantive response with a supporting receipt can complete the path.

06 · Reuse

Check v7, then v8.

The completed memory exposes one representative omission in v7 and recognizes its repair in v8.

What to watch.

  1. Use Next or Play replay. The Evidence River compresses 432 records, 288 items, and 576 relationships into 13 legible clusters.
  2. Pause at Who Knows What. Read “Why this person?” and distinguish likely knowledge, evidence ownership, and authority to confirm.
  3. Compare the two answers. The vague answer leaves the node hollow; the source-backed answer adds an attributed receipt and closes it.
  4. Continue to the paper check. v7 moves the representative path to HOLD FOR REVIEW; corrected v8 returns it to READY for the loaded demo evidence—not legal approval.

Guided walkthrough

If you have two minutes, follow this route.

Formation → recovery → proof.

The two views tell one causal story. Start with the calmer visual explanation, then open the denser operational proof.

  1. 01

    Let the Growth Map fill.

    Notice that new records can reinforce existing lines as well as create new decisions. Follow the flow from Decisions to Risks, Promises, and Money.

  2. 02

    Highlight the hollow gap.

    This is the handoff between views: the network has preserved enough context to describe what it does not yet know.

  3. 03

    Open the Recovery Explorer.

    Step through the receipts and ranked experts. The system does not contact Maya; it uses the historical routes she helped create to find another appropriate source.

  4. 04

    Watch evidence change the state.

    The vague answer does nothing. The source-backed answer is attributed, completes the path, and leaves behind a reusable investigation trail.

  5. 05

    Finish with v7 and v8.

    The Explorer checks whether one representative remembered promise reached the paper. The point is the reusable mechanism—not that this is the only important commitment in the deal.

Honest demonstration boundary

The behavior is demonstrated. The outside world is not contacted.

Every person and receipt is synthetic.

Project Asterline, the $380M financing, its organizations, evidence, and terms were invented for this prototype.

The hosted experience is deterministic.

A fixed local fixture makes the replay repeatable, reviewable, and safe to share publicly.

No live operation happens here.

The public replay has no live enterprise connectors, sends no outreach, and performs no EverOS Cloud write.

Now choose how you want to see it.

Use the Growth Map for the clearest visual explanation. Use the Recovery Explorer for the complete, evidence-rich sequence.
