The hackathon question

What survives when the person who connected everything is gone?

Maya is already gone on Monday. A few-hundred-million-dollar financing closes Friday. The prototype follows evidence as it becomes decisions, risks, promises, and money; reveals a hollow path; reconstructs who should know and why; captures a sourced answer; and reuses the completed memory in a document check.

The product design treats EverOS as the load-bearing memory layer for attributed receipts and reusable investigation recipes. The public build below is intentionally safer and narrower.

What you are seeing

This hosted experience is a deterministic demonstration of the product idea—not a claim that live systems or people were contacted during the replay.

Entirely synthetic

The deal and every receipt are invented.

Project Asterline, its people, organizations, evidence, and financing terms exist only for this demonstration.

Deterministic replay

The browser runs a fixed local fixture.

That makes the visual sequence repeatable and keeps the demonstration available without private data or credentials.

No live operations

No connectors, outreach, or cloud write occurs here.

The hosted replay does not contact enterprise sources, reach a person, or write to EverOS Cloud.
