## Presenter guardrails

- Maya is already gone. Never show or describe an exit interview, call, message, meeting, referral, answer, or post-departure action from her. Her name may appear only on historical provenance or relationship edges.
- Give the Evidence River and compression the first visual beat. The 430 records are stored, preload-ready fixture metadata—not proof of a bulk load into a live EverOS space. Only v7 and v8 are staged live; never imply 432 live writes.
- Treat the 20-message, 10-episode, 5-profile EverOS read as inference-only runtime observation. Do not use it to support fixture facts, deal sources, map states, or readiness, and do not predeclare its concept labels.
- Keep **Who Knows What** in a side panel. It is not a fifth region. Do not collapse `likely_knows`, `can_provide_evidence`, `can_certify`, `authorized_to_confirm`, and `authorized_to_approve` into one confidence score.
- Label the Ada exchange **Prepared simulated call · no real call placed**. Do not imply real dialing, calendar, meeting, or messaging activity. Keep transcript lines synchronized with provenance and offer the silent transcript fallback.
- Keep the item open after Ada's vague answer. Only her substantive sourced response plus Ren's approved internal record confirms it.
- Describe the omission as **one selected example among 144 contract-scoped items**, never as primary, critical, central, highest-risk, uniquely important, or a hero dependency.
- Render the v7 mismatch with a broken line and **Remembered, but missing from draft**; color alone is insufficient.
- `CONFIRMED` is not truth, `IN CONTRACT` is not enforceability, and `READY` is not legal approval or permission to close or fund.
- Do not mention out-of-scope vendors or integrations.
