# Deal Witness — Three-Minute Demo Script

> Synthetic demo only. All names, entities, events, and deal terms are invented and are not modeled on any actual company, transaction, or person.

| Time | Screen direction | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | Open **Project Brassleaf**. Show `$24M`, `Closes Friday`, `Maya leaves tomorrow`, and status `READY`. | “It is Monday. This synthetic $24 million loan closes Friday, and Maya—the deal lead carrying the context—leaves tomorrow. The team taking over needs to know not just what was decided, but why.” |
| **0:20–0:45** | Reveal the 12-concept map in the fixed regions **Money, Promises, Risks, Decisions**. Keep every source hidden until requested. | “Deal Witness turns five kinds of scattered evidence into one small, plain-English map. These are concepts, not document counts, and each one has a path back to where we learned it.” |
| **0:45–1:15** | Select **Owners need permission to take cash out**. Animate the same node from `HEARD` after the call to `CONFIRMED` after the approved memo; attach the internal message and Maya's exit answer without adding nodes. | “On the negotiation call, we heard one protection. The approved memo then confirmed it. Later evidence adds support to the same remembered point—it does not inflate the map with duplicates. This is knowledge getting better supported, not merely more numerous.” |
| **1:15–1:35** | Open the short call, memo, and exit-answer excerpts beside the node. Show the exact plain-language promise. | “A covenant is simply a promise that protects the money. Here it means: owners cannot take cash out of the business without lender permission.” |
| **1:35–2:05** | Check **Closing draft v7**. Briefly show matched connections for the amount, uses of money, monthly numbers, new-debt limit, and insurance. Leave a visible gap or broken line from the confirmed critical promise to v7, labeled **Remembered, but missing from draft**. Change status `READY → HOLD FOR REVIEW`. | “Now the closing draft arrives. Most remembered terms are present, but this critical promise is not. Deal Witness keeps the memory confirmed, shows the broken contract connection, and stops the review: hold for review.” |
| **2:05–2:30** | Open the hold reason. Show the four memory sources on one side and the complete v7 promise section on the other. Do not suggest replacement wording. | “The system does not call the promise false, invent language, or make a legal judgment. It says exactly what happened: a required promise is supported by the record but missing from this draft.” |
| **2:30–2:55** | Check **Corrected draft v8**. Highlight section 4.4, repair the connection, move the node to `IN CONTRACT`, mark the blocker resolved, and return status to `READY`. | “The corrected draft adds the agreed promise. The current contract now matches the remembered decision, the connection is repaired, and the deal returns to ready.” |
| **2:55–3:00** | End on the full map and the repaired evidence path: `HEARD → CONFIRMED → IN CONTRACT`. | “Maya leaves. The deal remembers, and the corrected draft matches.” |

## Presenter guardrails

- Say `HOLD FOR REVIEW` with spaces in the UI; the fixture uses `hold_for_review` only as its machine ID.
- Do not describe `READY` as legal approval, `CONFIRMED` as truth, or `IN CONTRACT` as enforceability.
- Keep the critical promise as one concept throughout the animation.
- Render the v7 mismatch with both a broken-line treatment and a text label; color alone is insufficient.
- Do not mention implementation vendors or out-of-scope integrations in this product-story demo.
