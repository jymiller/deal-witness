## State language

- **HEARD** — captured from an initial source and not yet confirmed.
- **CONFIRMED** — supported by an approved source, explicit human confirmation, or a direct result from checking the current draft; not a legal judgment.
- **IN CONTRACT** — found in the current checked draft; not a judgment about enforceability.
- **MISSING** — remembered and supported, but absent from the current checked draft.
- **NOT REQUIRED** — contextual knowledge that is not expected to appear in the contract.
- **READY** — no unresolved blocker exists in the evidence loaded so far; not permission to close.
- **HOLD FOR REVIEW** — a required point is missing from the checked draft, so a person must review it.

## Canonical critical-promise copy

Always show the user-facing promise exactly as written below:

> Owners cannot take cash out of the business without lender permission.
