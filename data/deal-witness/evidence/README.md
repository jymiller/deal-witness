Nothing here describes or is modeled on a real airport, transaction, company, person, publication, community, or public statement. This pack is not legal or financial advice.

This pack summarizes a deterministic nine-month corpus for a fictional **$380 million private-credit financing** for runway and supporting-infrastructure expansion at the invented Asterline Gateway. Maya Soren, a long-tenured senior deal lead at the fictional Harbor Glass Private Credit, has already left when recovery begins Monday; the deal is targeting Friday close. She appears only through historical provenance and relationship edges showing what she noticed and connected over years. There is no exit interview, last-minute handoff, or new question to Maya.

The corpus contains 432 logical evidence records. These seven Markdown files are short, human-readable bundle views; they do not expand every generated record.
